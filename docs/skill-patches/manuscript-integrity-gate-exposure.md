## AI-generated formalization exposure (arXiv:2610.08144)

When a manuscript's verification story rests on an AI-generated Lean (or other
formal) artifact, compilation of that artifact does not certify the
manuscript's argument. Flag the exposure and require the M1–M3
statement-fidelity checklist (statement correspondence, argument
correspondence, credit honesty) before crediting the formalization. A
"verified in Lean" claim on an AI-generated formalization with no fidelity
check is an overclaim.
